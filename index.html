Four things worth knowing before you book

Straight from the salon's own booking notes, so there are no surprises at the desk.

A 5-day window on gel

If a gel service chips or lifts within five days, the nail fix is free. After that it is booked as a normal fix.

Fills within 21 days

After three weeks the product starts to lift and the stress point shifts, so a late fill is charged as a new set. Fills are only done on Rose Blvd work.

Book the removal with your set

A gel removal costs R70 with a new treatment instead of R80 on its own. Removals are never rushed, so allow the full time.

Plan nail art in advance

A 15-minute consultation costs R50 and lets you plan detailed designs with a technician. Book it together with your treatment.

Student? Your rubber base set is R250

The RB Student Club takes R100 off Rose Blvd's most booked treatment and cuts soft gel tips, acrylic tips and the Royal Treatment pedicure too.
