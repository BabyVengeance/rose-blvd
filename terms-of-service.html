4. Service guarantees and salon policies

5-day gel window

We use quality gel brands. If a gel service chips or lifts within 5 days of your appointment, we will fix it free of charge. After 5 days, repairs are charged as a nail fix. This does not limit your right under CPA section 54 to have a service that was not performed with reasonable care and skill remedied or refunded.

Fills

Fills are accepted within 21 days of your last set. After 21 days the appointment is charged and treated as a new full set, because lifted product can trap moisture and the nail's stress point has moved. We do not fill work done by other salons; a soak-off and new set are required.

Removals

Removals are priced lower when booked with a new treatment. Hard gel applied overseas takes about twice the time and product and is priced accordingly.

Nail fixes

A nail fix booking covers one nail. If more than one nail needs repair, call the salon first. If a single-nail booking turns out to need more work than the time allows, the salon will arrange another slot.

Consultations

The 15-minute consultation costs R50. It is non-refundable because it covers the technician's time. We try to keep the same technician for your consultation and treatment but cannot guarantee it on busy days.

Classic polish

Classic (non-gel) polish carries no chip guarantee. How long it lasts depends largely on aftercare.

Student Club

RB Student Club prices apply to the services listed in that category confirm proof of student status required.

5. Your health and informed consent

  • Tell your technician or therapist before treatment about allergies, skin conditions, diabetes or circulation problems, infections, recent sun exposure, medication (including retinoids and blood thinners) and pregnancy.
  • Laser hair removal, microneedling, dermaplaning, waxing and facials may require a consultation and a signed consent form. We may decline or postpone a treatment if it is not safe for you.
  • For laser, shave the area the night before, not on the day. Results vary between people and need a course of sessions.
  • Microneedling results build over 3 to 6 months and several sessions. Mild redness and sensitivity afterwards are normal; follow the aftercare you are given.
  • Our treatments are cosmetic. They are not medical treatment and do not replace advice from a doctor or podiatrist. Footlogix pedicures care for dry or cracked skin but do not treat medical foot conditions.
