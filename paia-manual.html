Last updated 17 November 2026

Information Officer: Information Officer name to be confirmed. Deputy Information Officer: name to be confirmed, if appointed. Postal and physical address as above. Requests should be marked for the attention of the Information Officer.

3. The Information Regulator's guide

The Information Regulator has published a guide on how to use PAIA and POPIA (section 10 of PAIA). It is available in all official languages from the Regulator: Woodmead North Office Park, 54 Maxwell Drive, Woodmead, Johannesburg, 2191; email enquiries@inforegulator.org.za; website www.inforegulator.org.za. A copy may also be inspected at our main address during opening hours.

4. Records automatically available

7. Processing of personal information

Purpose

Providing nail, skin, laser, brow, waxing and massage treatments; managing bookings; keeping clients safe; employment; accounting and legal compliance; and marketing to clients who opted in.

Categories of data subjects and information

  • Clients: name, contact details, appointment history, payment records, health information disclosed for treatment safety, design notes and photographs (with consent).
  • Children under 14: name and treatment, provided by a parent or guardian.
  • Employees and job applicants: identity numbers, contact details, banking details, qualifications, payroll and tax information.
  • Suppliers: company details, contact persons, banking details.

Recipients

Fresha (booking and payments), Meta platforms (WhatsApp messaging), accountants and auditors, payroll providers, banks, SARS and other authorities where required by law.

Cross-border transfers

Some operators store information outside South Africa. Transfers are made only under the conditions in section 72 of POPIA.

Security measures

Access-controlled booking software, role-based staff access, locked storage for paper forms, staff confidentiality undertakings and breach-notification procedures.

8. How to make a request

  1. Complete Form 2 (Request for Access to Record) prescribed under the PAIA Regulations, available from the Information Regulator's website or from us.
  2. Send it to the Information Officer by email or by hand to our main address, with a copy of your identity document. If you request on behalf of someone else, include proof of your authority.
  3. Describe the records in enough detail to identify them, say how you want access (copy, inspection or electronic), and state the right you want to exercise or protect.
  4. We will decide within 30 days and tell you in writing. This period may be extended once by up to 30 days in the circumstances set out in section 57 of PAIA.

To request your own personal information under POPIA section 23, you may simply email the Information Officer; no fee is charged to confirm whether we hold your information.

9. Fees

Fees are those prescribed in the PAIA Regulations as amended. A request fee may apply to requests for records other than your own personal information, and an access fee covers reproduction, search and preparation time beyond the free period. A deposit may be required where preparation takes longer than the prescribed time. You will be told the amount before any work begins.

10. Grounds for refusal and remedies

We may refuse access on the grounds in Chapter 4 of Part 3 of PAIA, including protecting another person's privacy, commercial information of a third party, confidential information, the safety of individuals and property, legally privileged records and our own commercial information. If your request is refused, you may lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator within 180 days or apply to court.

11. Availability of this manual

This manual is available on this website and at our main address during opening hours, free of charge.
