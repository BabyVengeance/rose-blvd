The people behind the Rose Blvd set

Twenty-seven technicians and therapists across three salons. Clients often book them by name. Look for the pink neon on the wall: every portrait here was taken in front of it.

Where it started

Rose Blvd began on Main Road in Sea Point. The salon describes it as "the heart of our brand and where it all began", and with more than 5,000 reviews it is still the most reviewed of the three.

There are now two more. Regent Road sits among Sea Point’s restaurants and bars and adds Korean skincare facials, microneedling, laser hair removal and massage to the nail menu. Claremont is on Vineyard Road, just outside Cavendish Square.

The look carries through all three: blush walls, gold fittings and velvet pedicure thrones. So does the menu, priced the same wherever you sit.
