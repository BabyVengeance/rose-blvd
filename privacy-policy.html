Privacy policy

Last updated 17 November 2026

Draft for legal reviewPrepared for Rose Blvd Beauty Bar from public information. Items marked like this must be completed by the business, and the final text should be checked by a South African attorney before publication.

This policy explains how Rose Blvd Beauty Bar ("Rose Blvd", "we", "us") handles personal information when you visit our salons, book an appointment, contact us or use this website. We process personal information in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA) and, for visitors from the European Union or United Kingdom, the General Data Protection Regulation where it applies.

3. Why we use it and our lawful basis

  • To book, provide and manage your treatments, including reminders and rescheduling: necessary to carry out our agreement with you (POPIA section 11(1)(b)).
  • To treat you safely, using health information you give us before skin, laser, waxing or massage treatments: with your explicit consent (POPIA sections 26 and 27(1)(a)), and to establish or defend legal claims.
  • To answer enquiries and complaints: our legitimate interest in running the business, and your request.
  • To keep financial records as required by tax and company law: legal obligation (section 11(1)(c)).
  • To send offers or news: only if you opted in (section 69). Every message tells you how to opt out.
  • To post photos of your nails: only with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

4. Children

We provide services to children under 14 only when a parent or guardian books and consents on their behalf (POPIA section 35). We collect no more than the child's name and the treatment booked, and we do not photograph children for publication.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with operators who process it on our behalf under written terms, and only as far as needed:

  • Fresha, our booking and payment platform. Fresha also acts as a responsible party for your Fresha account; see Fresha's own privacy policy.
  • Meta (WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook) when you message us on those platforms.
  • Our website host, accountants and IT support.
  • Authorities where the law requires it.

Some of these providers store information outside South Africa. Where they do, we rely on POPIA section 72: the recipient is bound by laws or agreements giving protection similar to POPIA, or you have consented.

7. How we protect it

Booking data sits in Fresha's access-controlled system. Paper consent forms are kept in a locked cabinet at each salon. Staff access only what they need for your appointment and are trained on confidentiality. If a security compromise affects your information, we will notify the Information Regulator and you as soon as reasonably possible, as required by POPIA section 22.

8. Your rights

Under POPIA sections 23 to 25 you may:

  • ask whether we hold information about you and request a copy (see our PAIA manual);
  • ask us to correct or delete information that is inaccurate, out of date, excessive or unlawfully obtained;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests, and to direct marketing at any time;
  • withdraw consent, without affecting processing that already took place;
  • lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator.

Send requests to the Information Officer at privacy email address to be confirmed. We will reply within 30 days. We may ask you to confirm your identity first.

9. Cookies and this website

This website does not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. It loads fonts from our own server. The salon location pages embed Google Maps, which may set Google cookies when the map loads; see Google's privacy policy. Booking links take you to Fresha, which has its own cookie notice.

10. Information Regulator

Information Regulator (South Africa), Woodmead North Office Park, 54 Maxwell Drive, Woodmead, Johannesburg, 2191. Complaints: POPIAComplaints@inforegulator.org.za. General enquiries: enquiries@inforegulator.org.za. Website: www.inforegulator.org.za.
