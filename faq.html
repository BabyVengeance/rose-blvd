Booking and visiting

How do I book an appointment?

Book on Fresha for live availability and an instant confirmation. You can choose your salon, treatment and technician there. If you would rather talk to someone, WhatsApp or call the salon: Main Road on 069 354 5285, Regent Road on 066 313 1966 and Claremont on 069 330 7721.

Are prices the same at every salon?

Yes. The nail menu and prices are identical at Main Road, Regent Road and Claremont. Regent Road also offers Korean skincare facials, microneedling, laser hair removal, brows, waxing and massage, and sculpted acrylic is done at Claremont and Regent Road.

What are your opening hours?

All three salons open Monday to Friday from 09:45 to 18:30, Saturday from 09:15 to 17:00 and Sunday from 09:15 to 16:00.

Can I book for a group, a birthday or a bridal party?

Yes. Fresha takes group appointments for smaller groups. For larger groups or a set time for everyone, send an enquiry through the group booking form and the salon will reply on WhatsApp.

Do you do children’s nails?

Yes. The Little Rosebud jelly manicure (R115) and pedicure (R130) are for children under 14: a jelly soak, gentle scrub, massage and classic polish in their favourite colour. A parent or guardian makes the booking.

How can I pay?

The salons accept cash, credit cards and debit cards.

Nails and aftercare

What happens if my gel chips?

Every gel service has a 5-day window. If your gel chips or lifts within five days, the salon fixes it for free. After that, book a nail fix: from R20 for gel up to R60 for polygel.

How long can I leave it before a fill?

Fills are done within 21 days of your last set. After three weeks the product starts to lift, which can trap moisture, and the stress point of the nail has moved, so the appointment is charged as a new set.

Will you fill nails done at another salon?

No. To keep to their own standard, the technicians soak off work from other salons and apply a new set.

Should I book my removal separately?

Book it with your new treatment. A gel removal on hands is R70 with a new set and R80 on its own; acrylic is R85 instead of R100. Removals are not rushed, so allow the full time shown.

Rubber base, Supreme base or BIAB: what is the difference?

All three go over your natural nail. Rubber base is a flexible base that adds strength and is the salon’s most booked treatment (R350 with gel colour). Supreme base comes in clear, nude, milky white or milky pink (R370 with gel colour). Bio Sculpture builder gel (BIAB) gives more structure without length (R400). If you are unsure, ask your technician or book a R50 consultation.

Can I bring a design I found online?

Yes. For detailed art, book the 15-minute consultation (R50, non-refundable) together with your treatment so the same technician plans and does your set. For gel ombre in colours other than pink-to-white, WhatsApp the salon first to check stock.

What should I wear to a pedicure?

Open shoes or sandals, or bring a pair of flip flops, so your polish is not smudged on the way out.

Does classic polish last?

Classic polish can last a few days without chipping, but there is no guarantee and it depends on how you care for it. Choose gel if you need it to last.

What is the RB Student Club?

Student prices on six popular services, including rubber base with gel polish for R250 instead of R350 and soft gel tips for R300 instead of R400. Book them under the RB Student Club category on Fresha.

Skin, laser and massage at Regent Road

How do I prepare for laser hair removal?

Shave the area the night before your appointment, not on the day, as same-day shaving can irritate the skin. Shaving is optional for facial areas. Start with the free 20-minute consultation, and ask about courses of 6, 9 or 12 sessions on 060 988 7419.

Which facial should I book?

The Nevaeh Hydro Facial (R590) is for skin that feels tight or dry. The Essence (R630) calms sensitive or sun-exposed skin. The Detox (R670) suits oily or congested skin. The Glow (R700) uses the 24K Gold Jelly Mask for radiance. The Luminous (R540) is a dermaplaning facial for smooth skin before an occasion.

What should I expect from microneedling?

A numbing cream is applied first. Results build over several sessions and are most visible 3 to 6 months later. The Nevaeh Skin Renewal Package (R1,350) adds a Hydro Facial 5 to 7 days after your session to support healing. Tell your therapist about any skin condition, medication or pregnancy before treatment.

Do you offer massages?

Yes, at Regent Road: Swedish and aromatherapy massages from R480 for back, neck and shoulders to R690 for a full-body Swedish massage, and a R290 scalp and neck massage.
